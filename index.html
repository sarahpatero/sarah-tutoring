About me

Hi, I'm Sarah.

I tutor and mentor students one on one. The tutoring part is what parents usually reach out about. The mentoring part is usually what ends up mattering most.

I've always been the friend people feel comfortable around, the one people open up to easily, and the one who somehow gives decent advice, mostly because I actually try to live it myself. I also happen to be academically inclined. I'm currently excelling in three majors in college, but that was never really the point for me. What I've cared about for as long as I can remember is helping people become the best version of themselves. I think that started back in my own childhood.

I've had mentors in my life who completely changed the direction I was headed. I would not be who I am without them, and I would be honored to be that for your child too.

So that is how I show up in every session. We work through the actual material, and we also work on the things underneath it: confidence, discipline, wellbeing, mindset, communication, and professional habits that carry a student well past a single class.

My academic background is in actuarial mathematics, economics, and psychology at the University of Michigan. Outside of school I have spent years in color guard and dance, work as a certified barre instructor, and have been a resident advisor, which is really just years of practice keeping people motivated, comfortable, and moving forward even when they do not feel like it.

Mentorship

Mentoring the full student

This is the part of my work I care about most. It shows up in every session, whether we are reviewing a test or talking through a rough week.

01

Confidence & mindset

Most students who say they are bad at something are really just used to being stuck in front of other people. We rebuild that, and work on the story they tell themselves about their own ability.

02

Discipline

Showing up, starting before you feel ready, and finishing the thing. We set small systems that survive a bad week instead of relying on motivation.

03

Communication

Asking a clear question, speaking up in class, emailing a teacher, having a hard conversation. Skills that make everything else easier once they are in place.

04

Professional development

Deadlines, calendars, resumes, interviews, and how to carry yourself in a room. I teach it at whatever level actually fits the student.

05

Health & fitness

Sleep, nutrition, and movement, drawn from my own background as a barre instructor. A student running on empty cannot learn, so we talk about it honestly.

06

Color guard & dance

For students who want coaching in movement and performance too. I bring years in color guard and dance, and everything that teaches about discipline and poise.

How I work

How sessions actually go

Every student gets their own plan and their own materials. Nothing here is recycled from the last kid.

01

Explain it back to me

I do not move on until a student can say it in their own plain words. Repeating the steps is not the same as understanding them.

02

Built for that student

Lessons are made for the person in front of me, at their pace, from catching up on old gaps to going well past grade level.

03

Real accountability

Consistent sessions, honest updates for parents, and a plan that changes as the student grows. I will tell you what I am actually seeing.
